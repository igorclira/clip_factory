# Style Guide — GTA 6 Chaos & Moments (English / Global)

## Format & Duration
- Target runtime: **62 to 70 seconds** (strictly >60s for TikTok Creator Rewards).
- Structure: **4 to 5 fast-paced clips** (10–16s each) with rapid transitions.

## Hook & Voiceover Rules (0:00–0:02)
- Open mid-chaos or right before impact.
- Keep narration minimal and punchy (5–10 words max) to set up the absurdity (e.g., *"GTA 6 NPCs have zero survival instinct"*). Let the physics and reactions breathe.

## Visual Pacing & Zooms
- Tone: Dry comedy, absurd physics/ragdolls, unhinged NPC behavior, police chase fails.
- Hard cuts on impact/punchlines (cut 0.5s after the laugh or crash, never linger on dead air).
- Zoom targets: NPC faces, absurd ragdoll physics, wanted-level stars, or sudden vehicle collisions.

## Memes, SFX & Audio (CRITICAL COPYRIGHT RULE)
- **NEVER** allow in-game car radio music in the final render (instant mute/strike risk).
- If a viral clip has car radio audio, flag `isolate_vocals: true` so the pipeline strips the music and lays royalty-free SFX/music underneath.
- No pure unedited story cutscenes or spoilers without transformative commentary.
