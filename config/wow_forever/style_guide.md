# Style Guide — WoW Forever Clips (English / Global)

## Format & Duration
- Target runtime: **62 to 70 seconds** (strictly >60s for TikTok Creator Rewards).
- Structure: **3 to 4 tight clips** per montage (14–22s each) linked by a theme (e.g., *"3 Worst Raid Wipes This Week"*, *"Unluckiest Loot Rolls"*).

## Hook & Voiceover Rules (0:00–0:03)
- Zero intro logos or greetings. Open immediately on peak tension or a 1-sentence hook (<12 words).
- **NEVER** merely describe what is visible (*"Look at his HP drop"*). Always add WoW mechanic/raid context (*"He popped Recklessness right before a cleave and got one-shot"*).

## Visual Pacing & Zooms
- No static gameplay shot longer than 3.5 seconds without a micro-zoom, caption pop, or UI focus.
- Zoom targets: Health bars, raid frames, threat meters, debuff icons, loot roll windows, or streamer facecam reactions.
- Layout: `1080x1920` vertical. Sharp 16:9 gameplay centered (or split-screen with UI zoom on top), blurred gameplay background (no black bars).

## Memes, SFX & Audio
- Tone: Escalating tension -> sudden disaster -> comedic punchline.
- Use audio ducking (`-14dB` on game/streamer audio) whenever the narrator hook speaks.
- Audio safety: Flag any clip with copyrighted Spotify/background music (`isolate_vocals: true`) before rendering.
