### 1. Creator Credit Badge & Framing

* **Screen Position:** Top-left corner of the 16:9 frame (`X: ~60px, Y: ~70px` from outer edge), positioned clear of the GTA HUD radar/minimap.
* **Layout & Geometry:**
  * Left anchor: Rectangular solid accent badge in deep orange/red displaying a white silhouette controller/trophy gamer icon.
  * Right body: Translucent smoky black rounded rectangle (`rgba(15, 15, 15, 0.75)`) extending horizontally to dynamically fit username character length (e.g., `LL14_ELITE` at 00:04.500, `SPECIALIST-END-8306` at 00:34.500, `XTREMEALMOND` at 02:12.500).
* **Typography:**
  * Font: Ultra-bold sans-serif geometric display font (similar to *Impact* / *Chalet London 1960* / *Bebas Neue*).
  * Color: Clean white (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle 1–2px soft dark drop shadow for separation against bright daytime sky and dusty terrain.
  * Casing: 100% Uppercase.
* **Animation In / Out:**
  * **In:** Snappy horizontal slide-in from left (`~150ms`) combined with a quick scale-pop (`105% -> 100%`).
  * **Out:** Quick slide-out left or smooth opacity fade-out (`~200ms`).
* **Duration:** Persists for the introductory **4.0 to 5.5 seconds** of every clip to establish ownership without cluttering the screen when the climax occurs.

### 2. Subtitle & Typography Design

* **Visual Identity & Styling:**
  * **Font & Weight:** Heavy, bold geometric sans-serif (*Futura Bold / Montserrat ExtraBold / Impact*).
  * **Stroke & Outline:** Thick outer black stroke (`6px to 8px`) with zero blur, ensuring 100% legibility over explosions, white skies, or dark night environments.
  * **Drop Shadow:** Hard black drop shadow (`distance: 3–4px, opacity: 90%`) placed directly under the stroke.
  * **Color Hierarchy:**
    * Default dialogue/statement: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
    * Punchline/Reaction emphasis: High-contrast yellow (`#FFE600`) or red (`#FF2A2A`) (e.g., `"PREPARE TO DIE"` at 00:59.500 in red; `"TARGET LOCKED"` at 00:46.500 in red outline).
  * **Screen Positioning:** Placed mid-screen or directly on/above the moving object/character involved rather than locked to the lower third, tracking moving vehicles and players.
* **Editorial Subtitling Rules (What Gets Subtitled vs Omitted):**
  * **Subtitled:** Vocal spikes, clutch voice-chat reactions, panicked screams (`"AAAHHHHH"` at 00:56.500), ironic streamer reactions (`"NO WAY"` at 00:20.500, `"BE SERIOUS"` / `"I AM SERIOUS"` at 01:43.500–01:47.500), and editor comedic commentary cues (`"WAIT FOR IT"` at 01:31.500, 01:35.500, 01:41.500).
  * **Intentionally Omitted:** Routine GTA Online mission prompts at the bottom center (`"Deliver the trailer..."`, `"Deliver the forged documents..."`), ambient game dialogue, and background team chatter.
* **Animation & Grouping:**
  * **Grouping:** Strict 1–3 words per pop. Long sentences are never dumped on-screen.
  * **Motion:** Scale pop (`80% -> 115% -> 100%` across 4–6 frames) on the exact syllable peak of the audio waveform.

### 4. SFX & Music Architecture

* **Signature SFX Categories:**
  * **Comic Accents:** High-pitched cartoon slide whistles, classic goofy car horns (`*HONK*`), boing/spring sounds for small physics bumps.
  * **Tension & Release:** Sub-bass drops (808 boom), high-frequency cinematic risers on parabolic arcs (e.g., cars launching into the clouds).
  * **Impact Stingers:** Deep metallic clanks on vehicle bumper collisions, crisp call of duty hitmarker clicks (`tick/hitmarker.wav`) for precision snipes, and heavy bass-boosted thuds on sudden deaths.
  * **Meme Stingers:** Sarcastic dramatic brass stabs, record scratches on unexpected failures, and subtle audio ducking.
* **The "Zoom + SFX + Silence" Combo Formula:**
  1. *Build-Up:* Car boosts or flies upward; background game audio ramps up with rocket thruster white noise.
  2. *The Void (Silence):* 0.5s before impact, all game audio drops by `-12dB` to `-18dB`. A single high-pass riser or silence isolates the moment.
  3. *The Slam:* On contact, an explosive sub-bass hit + hitmarker audio triggers concurrently with a digital screen shake (`2–4 frame camera rumble`) and instant punch-zoom.
* **BGM Strategy:**
  * Compilations use upbeat, neutral electronic/funk/royalty-free trap grooves running at low background volume (`-20dB to -24dB`).
  * Music abruptly cuts out on comedic timing beats (e.g., Trevor cutscene failure at 01:25.000, cutting instantly to the fail tone and dead silence).

### 5. Clip Transitions & Pacing Rhythm

* **Clip Duration:** High-density pacing. Most clips run **6 to 14 seconds**. No dead air:
  * Hook / Setup: 1–3 seconds.
  * Action / Parabolic flight / Stunt: 3–6 seconds.
  * Punchline / Climax: 1–2 seconds.
* **The "Cut-on-Reaction" Outpoint Rule:**
  * The editor cuts to the next clip **0.5 to 1.0 second** after the punchline lands (e.g., the moment the car lands, the kill notification registers, or the player finishes a laugh). Lingering is forbidden.
* **Transition Mechanics:**
  * 85% **Hard Cuts** directly between gameplay clips on downbeats.
  * 10% **Whip Pans / Digital Swipes** paired with whoosh SFX to reset spatial orientation.
  * 5% **TV Static Glitch Wipe** (observed at 02:26.500 with classic SMPTE color bars + white noise static burst) used as an act break to reset pacing.

### 6. Clip Selection & Curation Criteria (GTA / Action Games)

#### High-Value Clip Archetypes Featured:
1. **The Parabolic Miracle (1-in-a-Million Precision):** Boosting into the stratosphere and accidentally/deliberately hitting another flying vehicle (e.g., Vigilante hitting Lazer at 00:41.000; Scramjet squashing helicopter at 01:43.000).
2. **The Physics Fluke / Near-Miss Survival:** Near-miss ragdoll sequences where player survival is determined by millimeters (e.g., monster truck jumping over player crawling on road at 01:02.000 and 02:06.000; jet skimming over crawling player at 01:14.000).
3. **The Absurd Mission Delivery:** Completing an objective via unintentional or hyper-stylized stunts (e.g., dropping out of a cargo plane cargo bay in a delivery van and landing flat in the mission ring at 02:23.000; falling into an empty chopper seat at 00:32.000).
4. **Instant Cutscene Disasters:** Narrative scripted moments interrupted by erratic sandbox physics (e.g., Ron walking into Trevor's helicopter rotor blades at 01:24.000 causing instant mission failure).

#### Instant Rejection Flags (What Red Arcade Discards):
* **Long setups:** Clips requiring more than 5 seconds of driving or running before anything unusual happens.
* **Generic kills:** Standard Oppressor Mk II lock-on kills or routine sniper headshots without unique physics interactions.
* **Low-resolution / cluttered HUD:** Clips with intrusive third-party watermarks, heavy twitch overlays blocking the center frame, or low framerates (<60fps).
* **Missing punchlines:** Stunts that look cool but end in an anticlimactic bounce or unconvincing ragdoll without a definitive visual resolution.
